The Problem
The art market has no reliable way to verify the physical identity of an artwork. Forgery, fractured provenance, and fragmented document workflows cost galleries, collectors, and auctioneers — with no reliable record that survives the next sale.
What I Did
Ran 60+ discovery interviews with collectors, galleries, and appraisers. Built the backlog and GTM from zero. Repositioned from mobile-first to web-first after usage data revealed galleries as the growth driver. Shipped the fingerprint verification system and co-pitched tier-1 auction houses directly.
Outcomes
iOS, Android, and web apps shipped before the six-month mark. Fingerprint registration live. 10+ gallery partnerships signed. Active pilots running with tier-1 Swiss auction houses. Core KPIs defined — PostHog dashboard now used by the full team.

Commercial Scale
Grew ARR 2× while lifting margins by 8 points
The connectivity line was capped at €7.5M because our global coverage had gaps that enterprise deals couldn't accept and our pricing model didn't fit the network we'd actually built. I mapped the coverage gaps, rebuilt pricing to match the expanded footprint, and restructured how we targeted verticals. That cleared the blockers. The line grew to €14.6M ARR — 2× in two years — with an 8-point margin improvement (14% → 22%) from pricing discipline and tighter cost management. At peak it was 56% of the company's total product-line revenue.

Automation Tool
Automated 4G/5G site design to cut delivery time and earn promotion
Site design meant running the same calculations — link budgets, neighbour lists, antenna parameters, coverage predictions — by hand for every site. I wrote an automation tool that handled all of it. Weekly output jumped 66%, the rollout pipeline moved faster, and I got the Huawei Future Star 2020 award and a promotion to Technical Project Manager for it.
