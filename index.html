SmartStamp AG

Head of Product  ·  formerly Founding PM

SmartStamp is an integrity layer for physical artworks and collectibles, built for the businesses that handle them. Each piece is registered under a microscope that records a fingerprint of its surface, and its provenance and documents are kept with that record. I joined as Founding PM at the pre-seed stage and led the delivery of the iOS and Android MVP in under six months, then became Head of Product, line-managing engineering and UX and reporting to the CEO. Alongside the product work, I prototyped features myself with Claude Code and Figma, so engineering had a working specification to build from.

The problem

The art and collectibles market has no reliable way to check that a physical object is the one its records describe. Provenance breaks when a work changes owners, and the papers that come with it are often lost in a sale.

What I did

Ran discovery interviews with collectors, galleries, and appraisers. Built the backlog and GTM from zero. Repositioned from mobile-first to web-first after usage data showed that galleries and auctioneers, not individual collectors, were driving adoption. Led the delivery of the fingerprint verification system and co-pitched to tier-1 auction houses.

Outcomes

The iOS and Android MVP shipped in under six months, and fingerprint registration went live. Gallery partnerships were signed and pilot talks opened with tier-1 Swiss and global auction houses. I defined the core KPIs and built the PostHog dashboard the team uses to track them.

How I planned the work

One rolling roadmap covered the next four months. Each month ran as a program increment (PI) with its own milestone, split into weekly sprints, and every sprint had three ceremonies: a start to set the objectives, a mid-sprint check and a finish to review the work. The month closed with a demo to the CEO, the board and the heads of department. What came out of the demo, together with the voice of the customer and the development effort still needed, decided how the roadmap rolled forward.

Commercial scale

Grew ARR 2× while lifting margins by 8 points

The connectivity line was capped at €7.5M because our global coverage had gaps that enterprise deals couldn't accept and our pricing model didn't fit the network we had built. I mapped the coverage gaps, rebuilt pricing to match the expanded footprint, and restructured how we targeted verticals, which removed both blockers. The line grew to €14.6M ARR, 2× over four years, with an 8-point margin improvement (14% to 22%) from pricing discipline and tighter cost management. At peak it was 56% of the company's total product-line revenue.

API quoting platform
