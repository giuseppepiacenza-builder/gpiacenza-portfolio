The Problem
The art market has no reliable way to verify the physical identity of an artwork. Forgery, fractured provenance, and fragmented document workflows cost galleries, collectors, and auctioneers, with no reliable record that survives the next sale.
What I Did
Ran 60+ discovery interviews with collectors, galleries, and appraisers. Built the backlog and GTM from zero. Repositioned from mobile-first to web-first after usage data revealed galleries as the growth driver. Shipped the fingerprint verification system and co-pitched tier-1 auction houses directly.
Outcomes
iOS, Android, and web apps shipped before the six-month mark. Fingerprint registration live. 10+ gallery partnerships signed. Active pilots running with tier-1 Swiss auction houses. Core KPIs defined. PostHog dashboard now used by the full team.

Commercial Scale

Grew ARR 2× while lifting margins by 8 points

The connectivity line was capped at €7.5M because our global coverage had gaps that enterprise deals couldn't accept and our pricing model didn't fit the network we'd actually built. I mapped the coverage gaps, rebuilt pricing to match the expanded footprint, and restructured how we targeted verticals. That cleared the blockers. The line grew to €14.6M ARR, 2× over four years, with an 8-point margin improvement (14% to 22%) from pricing discipline and tighter cost management. At peak it was 56% of the company's total product-line revenue.
Enterprise Logos

Renewed a ~€10B flagship customer and signed a ~€4B new logo

Both cycles were too large and too technical for sales to own alone, so I ran them jointly. The first was a flagship renewal: the customer had outgrown the original scope, so I rebuilt the roadmap case around their actual network direction and got it re-signed: a multi-million euro renewal with a ~€10B public company. The second was a new logo: I scoped a custom global coverage package from scratch and closed a new contract with a ~€4B public company.
API Quoting Platform

Revived a near-zero product line, then shipped the tooling to scale it

When I took ownership in 2021, the product line had stalled following a leadership transition. I rebuilt from scratch: conducted fresh market research, rewrote pricing and segmentation logic, and shipped an API-based quoting platform and, separately, real-time KPI dashboards. The quoting tool replaced spreadsheets and manual calculations; the dashboards gave leadership a live view of where deals stood. Both tools stuck and the team still uses them.
