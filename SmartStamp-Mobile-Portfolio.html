SmartStamp Mobile

The SmartStamp app is how galleries and collectors check an artwork or collectible in front of them. A scan session captures up to nine close-up images of the surface and compares them with the fingerprint recorded at registration. The app can also register a work and transfer its ownership.

Overview

The app covers the physical side of SmartStamp. Most works are registered under a microscope, and the app is then used to verify them: it guides the user through capturing the registered details again and checks them against the fingerprint on record. Each work has a digital passport in the app, with its SmartStamp ID, ownership record, surface images and documents. When a work changes hands, the current owner transfers it in the app, and the ownership record updates immediately in the web platform used by gallery staff. As Founding PM I defined the app from customer discovery onwards and led its delivery with the engineering team; the iOS and Android MVP shipped in under six months.

Registration and verification

Verification starts from a QR code or a SmartStamp ID. The app guides the user to the registered details, captures them again, compares them with the fingerprint on record and then shows the ownership details. Most works are registered under a microscope, but the app can also register a work in four steps: an overview photo, a close-up detail shot, the selection of fingerprint points on the surface, and submission to create the record.

Artwork record

The artwork detail view brings together the SmartStamp ID, the ownership record, the surface images and any attached documents. The Images tab shows the overview photo, the close-up detail and each fingerprint capture side by side, so anyone holding the work can compare it with the registered record without specialist equipment.

Ownership transfer

To transfer a work, the current owner enters the recipient's email address and confirms. A final warning states that the transfer cannot be undone. Once it is confirmed, the digital passport updates immediately and both parties receive an email with the transfer record. The previous ownership history stays in the record.

Documents and certificates

The app supports eight document types: Certificate of Authenticity, Condition Report, Appraisal, Insurance, Ownership, Provenance Record, Restoration Report and Other. Each document is attached to a specific artwork and stored in Firebase Storage. The Certificate of Authenticity is generated from the artwork's metadata and can be downloaded as a PDF without any other software.

Home screen

The home screen gives access to the four main actions: verify an artwork, create a space, transfer ownership and more options. Spaces group artworks by collection or location, and the Transferred section lists recent ownership changes. Data syncs from Firestore in real time, so gallery staff and collectors see the same records.

Architecture

The app is a single Flutter codebase for iOS and Android. Firebase handles real-time sync, authentication and file storage, PostHog tracks product usage, and Postmark sends the transactional email for ownership transfers and document sharing.

Flutter, single codebase
One Dart codebase ships to both iOS and Android. The UI is fully custom, without third-party component libraries, which gives full control over the interaction and visual design of every fingerprinting flow.
Firebase, real-time sync
Firestore keeps the mobile app and the web dashboard in sync with no polling. Authentication, storage and functions sit in the same project, so the two platforms share one infrastructure.
PostHog and Postmark
PostHog captures funnel data and feature usage without sending data to third-party ad networks. Postmark handles time-sensitive transactional email: transfer confirmations, document delivery, and status updates.
